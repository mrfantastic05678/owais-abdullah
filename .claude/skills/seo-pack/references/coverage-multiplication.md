# Coverage Multiplication

One keyword's research should produce more than one page.

## Page + video + shorts/social (simultaneous)
- For priority keywords, ship the web page, a YouTube video, and short-form variants **at the same time**.
- **Why:** Simultaneous publication compounds — each surface feeds the others' discovery. Staggered releases lose the compounding window.

## Video script seed per article
- Every brief includes: suggested video title + 3 talking points.
- **Why:** Phase 1 is web-first, but the seed makes future video production a fill-in exercise, not new research.

## Repurpose bundle
- Every article ships with: LinkedIn post, X thread, short-video script.
- **Why:** Distribution assets generated while the research is hot. Store now, distribute in Phase 2 / Factory upsell.

## Competitor brand-name targeting
- Create honest comparison content targeting competitor brand names ("[Client] vs [Competitor]").
- **Rule:** Fact-checked, neutral tone — the prospect searching the competitor's name meets your client's page first.

## PAA atomization
- High-value PAA questions get **dedicated pages**, not just accordion entries.
- **Why:** One question = one ranking/citation opportunity. Accordions consolidate; atomized pages multiply surface area.
- **Rule:** On-page FAQ stays for UX + schema; atomized pages capture the query-level traffic.
