Questions I get

FAQ

What exactly is a Digital FTE?

An AI agent set up to own a specific role in your business — with your SOPs, your tools, and defined outputs — rather than a chatbot that waits to be asked.

How long does an AI chatbot take?

A document-trained, embedded chatbot is typically live in days. Custom agent systems depend on scope — the spec phase tells us before you commit.

Do you hand over the code?

Yes. You get the repository, deployment access, and documentation. No lock-in.

Which LLMs do the agents run on?

We configure agents to use the model that best fits the budget and complexity. Usually OpenAI GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, or fine-tuned open-weight models for self-hosted instances.

Do you offer post-handover support?

Yes. Every deployment includes an optional SLA for routine monitoring, token cost optimization, and adjustments if your business SOPs change.
