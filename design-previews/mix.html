Diagnostics FAQ

What is a Digital FTE?

An AI agent configured to perform a set of repeatable workflows (reading files, calling APIs, filling forms) rather than a chatbot waiting for prompts.

Is the agent's code ours?

Yes. Upon final deployment, full repository rights and environment configurations are handed over. No vendor lock-in.

Where are the agents hosted?

We can host them on standard VPS platforms (Vercel, Railway, Dokploy) or directly inside your own private VPC environments.

Describe the business SOP. We compile the agent.
